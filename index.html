▸ HOW IT PLAYS ◂

Drop into a 10-second battle sprint and your real reps are counted live — every push-up, squat and star-jump lands a punch. Push-ups are read by the Face ID depth scanner; squats and star-jumps by the motion sensors. No camera? Tap-Only mode keeps everyone in the fight.

▶ THE CLIMB: Trade sweat for power. Bank the currency your reps generate, gear up, and evolve your hero through tier after tier — from couch potato toward god-like strength. A gauntlet of bosses stands in your way, and a secret form waits for those who go all the way. The rest, you'll have to play to find out.

★ A GAME THAT MUST BE FAIR ★

This game must be fair to everyone — including those less fortunate. There is no pay-to-win: your hero's evolution can never be bought — every tier is earned with real reps. The only purchase is an optional one-time supporter pack granting a small bonus of the same currency you earn by exercising — a thank-you head start, never a rank and never required. Every rank: 100% skill, 100% sweat.
